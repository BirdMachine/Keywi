Birdie's wishlist — these are goals, not a claim that they have shipped:

- Make theme exports easy to find: clear **Save to…**, a chosen folder and filename, and useful completion feedback.
- Offer **Share** after export, including Google Drive and other Android share targets; keep saving and sharing separately usable.
- Exercise theme import/export through local storage, Drive and other Android document providers, then verify export → fresh install/import → re-export.
- Capture real keyboard GIFs showing the on-tap and post-tap flash/color fade, plus videos with audible sound-customization examples.
- Explore [Nova-inspired tool-page transition effects and accessible navigation](https://github.com/BirdMachine/Keywi/issues/59), with ordinary tap access, optional holds, customization and reduced motion.
- Add emoji search and a better emoji pager, viewer and selector — a happier emoji life. 🩷
- Follow up on recent typing requests: ASCII quotes, suggestions following Shift, and strikethrough in the formatter if still absent.
