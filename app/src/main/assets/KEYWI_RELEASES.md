Keywi is your colorful keyboard playground, built on Thumb-Key's open-source foundation.

## Tool controls follow-up
- Feedback mode is a normal ↗ swipe preset on the emoji/tools key. The corner legend follows the mode; taps and other swipes keep their existing actions, and explicit Tasker assignments take priority.
- The toolbar cycler uses a diamond in a disk again. Swipe down advances and up goes back; **Toolbar controls → Invert tool scroll** reverses this, including while the keyboard is open.
- The advanced theme editor now shows active-theme settings artwork around its existing controls. Save/Revert, Chroma, gradients, fonts and key/group tools remain available; the stale planned-effects note now links to the working effects page.

## What's changed — October 24, 2026

### Sound, vibrate, or silence
- Tap the feedback icon in the emoji/tools key’s lower-right corner to cycle **Sound → Vibrate only → Silent**. Quick Settings has the same control for every cut, including HK-Like.
- Sound uses your enabled sound pack, or Thumb-Key’s standard Android click if custom sounds are off. Vibrate only suppresses all keyboard sounds; Silent suppresses sound and vibration. Pack selections are preserved.

### Color that lingers
- **Settings → Keypress effects → Key flash & drag trails** now controls fade-out duration and an ordered sequence of color frames.
- Add, edit, reorder, or remove frames with the Chroma Desk picker. Colors blend through the sequence as the accent fades. Clear the sequence to follow your theme accent; a zero duration turns the effect off.
- Optional drag trails share the sequence and fade time. GIF/PNG effects remain above the accent layer. Password fields hide visual typing effects.

### Bringing the recent work together
- Chroma Desk color disc with precise HEX/RGB inputs; Oklab Broad and Tight gradient presets.
- Theme artwork and sculpted settings surfaces, clearer key/group selection and fitted gradients.
- Kiwi toolbar page controls, Fancy Text, Kaomoji web search and Quick Settings.
- Tasker key actions and opt-in appearance automation; sound packs and per-action GIF/PNG overlays.
- Transparent launcher artwork and tighter main-settings text leading.

## What's changed — October 12, 2026

### Welcome, in full color
- Two gem-styled banners now sit at the top of settings: **Welcome to Keywi** and **Android installation policy**.
- Tap either banner to reopen its full overlay. Update notices appear once per installed build, one at a time.
- These release notes describe **Keywi**, rather than inherited Thumb-Key release notes.
- Thumb-Key's GitHub, Patreon and Liberapay links are explicitly labeled as upstream. Donations through those links support **Thumb-Key, not Keywi**.
- The installation-policy notice links to current Google documentation and independent advocacy without claiming that Keywi will automatically stop working.

### Recent Keywi improvements
- **Gem cuts:** compact previews use real keyboard geometry, with a selected-cut preview. Geometry, themes and character layouts stay independent.
- **Themes & appearance:** the advanced editor is prominent again, with gradients, images, GIFs, fonts, key surfaces and borders.
- **Theme packages:** each theme owns its media and fonts. Import/export bundles settings and assets together; migration preserves Custom edits and old JSON backups.
- **Current-word chip:** background and text/outline highlight colors sit together under Toolbar lozenges and save with the theme.
- **Settings navigation:** Themes & appearance sits below Gem cuts; overlapping legacy controls are grouped under their relevant advanced menus.
- **App icon:** the opalescent gem uses a transparent background.
